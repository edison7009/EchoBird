Pets and TokenPet

Turn tokens recorded through Smart Routes into pet growth, and learn about hatching, leveling up and supplies.

Start with a pet

Open Pets at the top of App Desktop. Six voxel pets are currently available in different colors: Iron Bean, Rainbow unicorn, Spined kaiju, Nine-tailed fox, Alien and Mr. Kangaroo. Select a pet, drag to rotate it, or adjust the viewing angle with the arrow keys.

TokenPet is a growth unit within the pet system. It is not an API balance, cannot be cashed out, and does not add usable quota with a provider.

Where TokenPet comes from

The system uses cumulative token consumption recorded in the Smart Routes ledger and credits only the increase to your pet growth balance, preventing the same cumulative usage from being credited twice. Enable Record statistics in Settings, and make sure upstream responses provide recognizable usage data.

Only recorded access usage counts

Consumption across all your websites, third-party tools and accounts is not automatically monitored or converted. Usage that does not pass through Smart Routes, or has no corresponding usable record, may not generate TokenPet. You do not need to spend extra model tokens to complete the beginner tutorial.

How to hatch and level up

  1. Check your TokenPet balance and select the pet you want to raise.
  2. Click Level up to put the available balance toward growth. If it is not enough for a full level, experience can accumulate within the current level.
  3. Wait for leveling and stage animations to finish before taking the next action. Watch the pet progress from an egg and cracked shell to a baby and adult.
  4. To raise more pets, buy a new egg at the price shown on the page. An egg currently costs 100 million TokenPet; use the displayed price and upgrade cost as your reference.

Growth stages

LevelForm
0–9Whole egg
10–19Cracked egg
20–29Baby
30–49Young
50–60Adult; the current maximum level is 60

What pet supplies do

  • Growth card:Adds TokenPet within the pet system.
  • Double card:Affects growth-card rewards for 15 minutes at present; it does not double model quota.
  • Haste card:Brings the next supply forward. It does not speed up model inference.

Check the page’s countdown for eligibility and the next supply time. Pets provide companionship alongside your work; they are not a prerequisite for using applications, models or Smart Routes.
